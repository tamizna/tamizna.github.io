Patuh OAT

Patuh OAT is an Android application aimed at supporting tuberculosis patients, doctors, families, healthcare workers, and psychologists. The application helps track patient medication adherence and provides educational resources about tuberculosis. Patients and their families can consult with doctors, healthcare workers, and psychologists about the patient’s treatment plan. Doctors can log treatment data, monitor patient adherence, and upload articles and videos to the application. Healthcare workers are responsible for assisting doctors in supervising patients and confirming daily whether the patients have taken their medication.
